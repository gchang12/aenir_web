Aenir

About

Wanna know how your Fire Emblem unit compares to the average?

Wanna know how the average stats of one unit compares to those of another?

You've come to the right place!

How to Use

Create a Morph

First you have to create a morph, a virtual copy of a unit.

  1. Specify the game the unit appears in.
  2. Specify the name of the unit.
  3. Toggle any other initialization parameters as necessary.

Evolve Morph

Now that you have your morph, it's time to simulate operations on it. Operations include:

  1. Levelling up.
  2. Promoting.

  3. Using stat boosters.

  4. Equipping scrolls.

  5. Using growths items.

  6. Shapeshifting.

Compare Morph

  • To compare a morph to your input stats, input your stats and click on the Preview button.
  • To compare a morph to another morph, select a morph-name from the drop-down menu.

Note: You'll have to sign in to keep your morph.
